# MA2001: Linear Algebra I

MA2001 Linear Algebra I, formerly coded as MA1101R before 2021, is a core module at the National University of Singapore with wide applications across various disciplines [source: ma2001-lecture-introduction-pdf#section-1]. This module serves as a first course in linear algebra, focusing on fundamental concepts within the context of Euclidean spaces $\mathbb{R}^n$. Students are expected to develop both computational skills and geometric intuition related to vectors and matrices [source: ma2001-ma2001-trial-run-2022-pdf#section-1].

## Module Structure

The module employs a blended learning approach, combining recorded video lectures, weekly online quizzes, and weekly live lectures. Recorded lectures cover textbook sections, and students must complete an online quiz after watching the videos. Live lectures are dedicated to reviewing previous topics, presenting additional examples, discussing problem-solving strategies, and Q&A sessions [source: ma2001-lecture-introduction-pdf#section-1]. Weekly laboratory sessions are also conducted in room S17-0302 to provide hands-on experience, often utilizing MATLAB [source: ma2001-lab-ma2001-lab-sessions-pdf#section-1].

## Key Topics

MA2001 covers a comprehensive range of linear algebra topics [source: ma2001-ma2001-trial-run-2022-pdf#section-1]:

*   **[[linear-systems-intro|Linear Systems and Gaussian Elimination]]**: Introduction to linear equations, solutions, and the process of [[gaussian-elimination-rref|Gaussian Elimination]] and [[gaussian-elimination-rref|Reduced Row Echelon Form (RREF)]] [source: ma2001-lecture-chapter1-pdf#section-1].
*   **[[matrix-operations|Matrices]] and [[determinants-properties|Determinants]]**: Definitions, types of matrices, [[matrix-operations|matrix operations]], [[invertible-elementary-matrices|invertible matrices]], and [[determinants-properties|properties of determinants]] [source: ma2001-lecture-chapter2-pdf#section-2].
*   **[[vector-spaces-subspaces-intro|Vector Spaces]]**: Introduction to [[vector-spaces-subspaces-intro|Euclidean n-Space]], [[linear-combinations-span|linear combinations]], [[linear-combinations-span|span]], [[linear-independence-bases|linear independence]], [[linear-independence-bases|bases]], and [[dimension-rank-nullity|dimension]] [source: ma2001-lecture-chapter3-pdf#section-1].
*   **[[row-column-nullspace|Vector Spaces Associated with Matrices]]**: Concepts of [[row-column-nullspace|row space]], [[row-column-nullspace|column space]], [[row-column-nullspace|nullspace]], [[dimension-rank-nullity|rank]], and [[dimension-rank-nullity|nullity]] [source: ma2001-lecture-chapter4-pdf#section-1].
*   **Orthogonality**: [[orthogonal-sets-projections|Inner products]], [[orthogonal-sets-projections|orthogonal sets and projections]], the [[gram-schmidt-process|Gram-Schmidt Process]], and [[least-squares-solutions|least squares solutions]].
*   **[[eigenvalues-eigenvectors|Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors]]**: Definitions, characteristic polynomials, [[diagonalization|diagonalization of matrices]], and their applications [source: ma2001-lecture-chapter6-pdf#section-1]. (Excluding quadratic forms) [source: ma2001-exam-information-pdf#section-1]
*   **[[linear-transformations-intro|Linear Transformations]]**: Introduction to linear transformations from $\mathbb{R}^n$ to $\mathbb{R}^m$, and the [[standard-matrix-linear-transformation|standard matrix of a linear transformation]] [source: ma2001-lecture-chapter7-pdf#section-1]. (Excluding geometric transformations) [source: ma2001-exam-information-pdf#section-1]
*   **MATLAB Applications**: Practical use of MATLAB for linear algebra operations, including [[matlab-basics|basic commands]], [[matlab-row-operations-geometry|row operations and geometric interpretations]], [[matlab-vector-spaces-bases|vector spaces and bases]], and [[matlab-matrix-subspaces-orthogonality|matrix subspaces and orthogonality]].

## Assessment

Assessment for MA2001 typically includes:
*   **Homework Assignments**: Regular assignments, such as Homework 1, 2, 3, and 4, each comprising several questions and contributing to the overall grade (e.g., 5 questions for 50 points per assignment) [source: ma2001-hw1-hw1-q-pdf#section-1, ma2001-hw2-hw2-q-pdf#section-1, ma2001-hw3-hw3-q-pdf#section-1, ma2001-hw4-hw4-q-pdf#section-1].
*   **Laboratory Assignment**: A lab-based assignment, typically consisting of 4 questions worth 60 marks, requiring students to apply MATLAB to linear algebra problems [source: ma2001-lab-assignment-a0251957u-lab-assignment-pdf#section-1].
*   **Final Examination**: A comprehensive examination covering all module topics (excluding quadratic forms and geometric transformations). Students are permitted one double-sided A4 helpsheet and a non-communicative calculator. The exam duration is 2 hours [source: ma2001-exam-information-pdf#section-1].
