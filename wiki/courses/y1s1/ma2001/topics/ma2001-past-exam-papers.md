# MA2001 Past Exam Papers

This page provides a collection of past examination papers for MA2001 Linear Algebra I (formerly MA1101R), designed to assist students in their revision and understanding of the exam format and question types.

For general information regarding the MA2001 examination, please refer to [[ma2001-exam-information]]. For revision materials, see [[ma2001-cheatsheets-revision]].

## General Examination Information

Across various semesters, several consistent instructions and policies have been observed for the MA2001 (MA1101R) final examinations [source:ma2001-finals-exam1415sem1-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1415sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1516sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1617sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1920sem1-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-1920sem2-no-ans-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-pastpapers-pdf#section-1]:

*   **Student Identification**: Candidates are typically required to write their matriculation/student number (and sometimes seat number) clearly on the top of each answer page, without writing their name [source:ma2001-finals-exam1415sem1-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1415sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1516sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1617sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1920sem1-pdf#section-1]. For some online exams (e.g., Sem 2, 2019/2020), specific instructions for writing on A4 paper (student number top left, question/page number top right) were provided [source:ma2001-finals-1920sem2-no-ans-pdf#section-1].
*   **Submission**: Answer booklets or scanned PDF documents are collected at the end of the examination [source:ma2001-finals-exam1415sem1-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1415sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1516sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1617sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1920sem1-pdf#section-1].
*   **Question Requirement**: All questions must be answered [source:ma2001-finals-exam1415sem1-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1415sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1516sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1617sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1920sem1-pdf#section-1].
*   **Calculators**: Scientific calculators are generally permitted, with an emphasis on systematically laying out all calculation steps [source:ma2001-finals-exam1415sem1-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1415sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1516sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1617sem2-pdf#section-1, ma2001-finals-exam1920sem1-pdf#section-1]. For the Semester 2, 2019/2020 exam, any calculator, including MATLAB, was allowed due to its open-book nature [source:ma2001-finals-1920sem2-no-ans-pdf#section-1].

## How to Use Past Exam Papers for Revision

Working through past exam papers is an effective way to:
*   Familiarise yourself with the types of questions asked and their typical weighting.
*   Practice time management under exam conditions.
*   Identify recurring topics and areas where you might need more revision.
*   Understand the expected depth of explanation and demonstration of steps.

Remember to attempt these papers under timed conditions, without referring to notes, to simulate the actual exam experience. Afterwards, review your answers thoroughly, paying attention to areas where you made mistakes or struggled.
